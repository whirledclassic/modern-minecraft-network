# Profit plan

Small Minecraft networks make money from **retention**, not from adding more game modes in week one.

## Legal fence (2026 reading)

Mojang usage guidelines allow cosmetics and convenience. They do not allow selling combat power or paid kits that beat free players in PvP.

Safe here:

- Chat prefix / color
- Extra homes
- Queue / identity
- Cosmetics later

Removed from the paid catalogue after review:

- VIP iron tools
- Champion survival flight

Starter tools are free (`/kit starter`). Everyone gets them.

Do not sell Minecraft itself. Do not claim you are official.

## Unit economics

- VPS 8 GB: about $20–40 / month
- Domain: about $12 / year

Break-even on hosting: ~8× VIP at $4.99 / 30 days.

Conversion on a decent small survival box is often 3–8% of weekly unique players.

## 90-day model

Days 1–14 private beta. Days 15–45 first 20 regulars + one weekly event. Days 46–90 cosmetics if the loop holds.

Do not add Skyblock and BedWars in month one.

Money flow: player pays off-site → pending order → Mark paid + grant. No card form in this repo.
